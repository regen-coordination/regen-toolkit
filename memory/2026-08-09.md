### Decisions

- **Ownership split by field, so nothing is owned twice.** `data/tasks.yaml` owns identity and
  routing; Linear owns status, assignee and cycle position; `HEARTBEAT.md` owns narrative.
  `apply` sends only repo-owned fields on update — otherwise the next sync would silently revert
  a reassignment someone made in Linear.
- **No fuzzy matching anywhere.** Idempotency comes from an exact committed id-map plus an
  empty-workspace preflight. **No delete path exists** in the tooling, not behind a flag.
- **`org-os-framework` is a proposal, not a team decision** — and it holds 86 of 172 issues,
  half the board. The team agreed to *hold* the org-os lane; nobody had quantified that this
  means holding half of all tracked work. Afo owns Linear structure and has not seen this yet.
  Projects are matched and adopted by name, so renaming is cheap.

## Incident — the seeded team was deleted and replaced (2026-08-09)

**All 172 seeded issues, 3 projects and 30 labels are gone.** Team `KCM` was renamed to
"Knowledge Commons Old" (`KCMO`) and archived at **05:15:19Z**, and a fresh empty `KC` created.
The issues were **deleted, not archived** — unreachable by UUID, by team filter, by the
`seed/repo` label, and org-wide with `includeArchived`. Only 4 issues exist org-wide, all in
`GROW`.

**Not caused by the tooling.** The complete apply log is `project.create` 3,
`project.link-initiative` 3, `label.create` 30, `issue.create` 172, `project.adopt` 3,
`issue.update` 20 — spanning 00:41:41Z to **00:47:45Z**, four and a half hours before the
deletion. No delete or archive mutation exists anywhere in `scripts/linear/`; that was a
deliberate constraint precisely so this question could be answered rather than argued.

**Cause:** almost certainly a team reorganisation by whoever owns Linear structure, done without
knowing 172 issues had just landed. The "tell Afo before seeding" coordination step had been
flagged repeatedly and was still outstanding when the seed ran.

### What this cost, and what it didn't

Nothing of value was lost. Linear was always the projection; `data/tasks.yaml` is the source of
truth, intact and validated. Re-seeding is one command.

The design handled its first real adverse event correctly, untested until now:
- the **orphan sweep** detected all 172 had vanished and marked them for recreation rather than
  assuming they existed;
- **preflight** passed on the empty `KC`;
- the plan then **refused to proceed** because 18 tasks target cycle 1 and cycles are disabled
  on the new team — it stopped rather than inventing a cycle.

### Blocked on

`cyclesEnabled: false` on `KC`. Enable it (Settings → Teams → KC → Cycles) before re-seeding, or
clear the `cycle` field on the 18 scheduled tasks. Note `cycleStartDay` defaults to Monday.

### Lesson

Seeding a shared workspace is an outward-facing act. The pilot-then-full approach limited the
blast radius of *technical* error well, but no amount of technical care substitutes for telling
the person who owns the structure. Do that before the re-seed.
